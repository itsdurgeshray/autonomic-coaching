The problem

Why do I still feel unwell when my labs are normal?

Your energy, sleep, focus or body composition is changing in ways you didn’t expect, even though the routines that used to work are still in place.

You’ve seen doctors, taken supplements, run tests and tracked your data, and nothing has explained it. Changes in your hormones are one possible explanation, and they’re easy to miss when each marker is read on its own.

What is Autonomic Wellness?

Autonomic Wellness gives you hormone care built around you, not around one test result. You complete an online assessment, and a licensed doctor, working with a scientist, turns your answers into personal recommendations in one consultation. It makes preventative care easier to afford and access.

How does Autonomic Wellness work?

  1. 01

    Assessment

    An online questionnaire covers symptoms, health history and lifestyle.

  2. 02

    Review

    Doctors and a scientist go through the answers before the consultation.

  3. 03

    Consultation

    A doctor sees you one-to-one.

  4. 04

    Recommendations

    The doctor sets out what is advised, including medical treatment if it’s needed.

Who leads your care?

Doctors lead the care, and Justin Maguire works alongside them. Personalized care takes both. The doctors make the medical decisions. The scientists reads your results against your own history, so the recommendations fit you and not just the average patient.
