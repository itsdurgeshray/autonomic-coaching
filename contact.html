You don’t need all the answers to begin

If your health, energy, or ability to manage pressure has changed, we’d like to understand what’s been going on. You may have had tests, tried different approaches, or received results that don’t explain how you feel.

Every request is treated with discretion.

What happens next

  1. 01

    Send your request

    Share what you’ve been experiencing by answering eight short questions about your health and goals. There’s no need for a complete medical history.

  2. 02

    Suitability consultation

    The team arranges a conversation about your history, symptoms, routines, and goals, supported by an intake form. It’s a chance to ask your own questions too.

  3. 03

    Your next step

    You may be invited to join the programme. If coaching isn’t the right next step, we’ll say so and help point you towards more suitable support.

The coaching commitment

If the consultation leads to an invitation, this is what the program involves.

Autonomic Coaching is one-to-one, led by Justin Maguire, a [] scientist. A collaborating licensed doctor handles medical matters.

Each month includes:

  • Four lifestyle coaching sessions
  • Two biopsychosocial reviews, where you score seven markers of how you’re doing
  • A dietary program, adjusted as you progress
  • WhatsApp support, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 CAT

Investigative reviews take place at the start of the program and every three months thereafter.

Programs run for 6, 12, or 18 months, starting at $11,111.11 USD, paid upfront. Lab tests, supplements, and prescribed treatments are quoted separately.
