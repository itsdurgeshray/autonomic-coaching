The first call and your Assessment

On the first call, we talk through what you’re dealing with and what you’ve already tried, and we decide together whether to go ahead.

If we do, the assessment begins. We work through four questions with you: what your symptoms are, which patterns sit behind them, what is driving those patterns, and what plan follows.

We work through four questions with you
  1. What your symptoms are
  2. Which patterns sit behind them
  3. What is driving those patterns
  4. What plan follows

We work from your blood work, genetics, hormones and symptom patterns.

We go through any lab results you already have, recommend further tests, and explain what each one shows. A lab result reads differently depending on how you’re sleeping, eating and coping with stress, so we look at those too, including your nervous system patterns and mental wellbeing.

What a typical week looks like

We meet once a week, one-to-one and online, so you can work with us from anywhere in the world. Between sessions, you fill in a short daily log of your sleep, food and how you feel. That way we work from what happened that week, not from memory.

In each session, we go through the log, see what changed, and decide what to adjust. We explain the reasoning behind each adjustment, and you make the decisions.

Travel, deadlines, training and stress all shift what your body needs, so the plan shifts with them, week by week instead of at a three-month check-in.

What coaching asks of you

Time

  • The weekly session, the daily log, and putting each adjustment into practice between sessions.
  • A change only counts once it’s part of your week, so most of the work happens outside the call.

Money

  • Coaching is a paid, private program that continues over months.
  • The work is built around you alone, from the first review of your history to each weekly session.

Emotional energy

  • Looking closely at your sleep, stress, relationships and habits.
  • Seeing those patterns plainly can be demanding.

Having people around you who support those changes makes them easier.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need lab tests to start coaching?

In most cases, yes. Blood work is the starting point for the assessment, and we recommend the tests that apply to you.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Therapy is treatment you receive, while coaching is learning you do. Some countries, including South Africa, regulate the two differently.

Does coaching replace my doctor?

No. Anything urgent should go to your own doctor or emergency services.
