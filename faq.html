Is Autonomic Coaching Right for You?

What coaching is, who it suits, and who it doesn’t.

What exactly is Autonomic Coaching?

It’s scientist-led, one-to-one health coaching. Justin Maguire and the team read your biology, psychology, and daily life together, using your symptoms, test results, and daily tracking, then turn what they find into a plan you can follow. A collaborating doctor handles anything medical.

Who is it for?

It’s for people whose sleep, stress, energy, digestion, or daily function has slipped and who haven’t found an explanation through doctors, programs, or supplements. The suitability consultation looks at your situation in detail. Symptoms that need medical assessment are referred on.

Who is it not for?

It isn’t for anyone who wants a diagnosis or a prescription from a coach. It also doesn’t suit people after a one-off assessment, help with a single issue, or a month-to-month arrangement.

Do I need a specific health condition to enroll?

No. You don’t need a diagnosis. Some clients arrive with a persistent concern, and others want a more structured, proactive approach to their health. If your needs are better met by medical care or another service, the team will tell you.

My blood work came back “normal.” Can coaching still make sense?

Laboratory reference ranges don't always tell the whole story of how someone feels. Your intake, test results, and monitoring data are reviewed together, so patterns a single test can miss have a chance to show up. No specific outcome is guaranteed.

How is this different from conventional healthcare?

Medical care and coaching do different jobs. A doctor assesses, diagnoses, interprets investigations, and makes treatment decisions. Coaching turns an agreed plan into daily habits and reviews your progress, looking at your physiology alongside stress, relationships and environment. The two work side by side.

Is Justin a doctor?

No. Justin Maguire is a scientist. He holds a BSc (Hons) in Nutritional Science and a Postgraduate Diploma in Functional Blood Chemistry Analysis, and he’s completing an MSc in Neuroscience and Psychology of Mental Health. He doesn’t diagnose or prescribe.

What if I don’t know what I need, or struggle to describe how I feel?

You don’t need every answer before you start. Structured questions, your own observations, and any data you have guide the conversation. If a session ever feels like too much, the pace and priorities are adjusted. If coaching isn’t the right fit, you may be directed to a medical consultation or another service.

Inside the Coaching Process

From your first consultation to the monthly rhythm.

How do I get started?

Coaching is by application. You send a short request, and the team arranges an initial consultation to check suitability.

What happens in the consultation?

You talk through your health history, symptoms, lifestyle, and goals, supported by an intake form. Afterward, you may be invited into the program, referred to another service or clinician, or advised not to enroll. It helps to bring any recent test results and a clear idea of the concerns you most want to address. You don’t need everything organized.

What happens once I’m accepted?

You review and sign your contract and itemized quotation before the first session, then complete the paperwork and onboarding. Your device setup and data sharing are agreed at onboarding. The baseline investigative review then sets your first coaching priorities.

What does the program include each month?
  • Four lifestyle coaching sessions
  • Two biopsychosocial coaching reviews
  • A dietary program, adjusted as you progress
  • WhatsApp support, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 CAT (acknowledged within one business day, not an emergency service)

Biopsychosocial reviews run on Monday or Friday and investigative reviews on Wednesday. You choose a time on Calendly. All times use Central Africa Time (CAT, UTC+2).

What are the seven markers?

At each biopsychosocial review, you score seven areas from 0 to 10: sleep and recovery, calm and stress recovery, energy and daily capacity, digestion, activity and exertion tolerance, mood, and participation in daily life. They’re tracking tools. They aren’t diagnostic tests.

Do I need new blood tests before starting?

Not necessarily. Existing results can be discussed in the initial consultation. Any further testing is decided by the collaborating doctor, and the testing needed and its cost are explained before anything is arranged.

Do I need devices?

The team recommends the Oura Ring 5, FreeStyle Libre 3 Plus, and Kinsa QuickCare for tracking sleep, glucose, and temperature. They aren’t included in the program fee. If you already own equivalent equipment, it may work. The Libre may need medical approval or a prescription where required.

Contract length, what’s included, and cancellation.

How long does the program run?

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the contract length and are in USD, paid upfront by EFT or bank transfer, Wise, crypto, or PayPal. You receive a written, itemized quotation before you enroll.

What isn’t included in the fee?

Lab panels and functional testing, supplements, prescribed medication, exercise equipment or gym membership, and shipping and import fees are quoted separately. Devices are bought separately. Your quotation also confirms physician fees, and any extra charge needs your agreement.

Can I cancel?

Send written notice to the team. The refund is the remaining prepaid value, less a cancellation charge capped at 50% of that remaining value. Where South Africa’s Consumer Protection Act applies, you may cancel on 20 business days’ notice.

Does coaching guarantee results?

No. Participation supports progress, but outcomes also depend on your health, circumstances, and response. No specific outcome is guaranteed.

Medical Oversight & International Access

How the medical side works, and joining from outside South Africa.

How does the doctor fit in?

A collaborating licensed doctor handles medical assessment, diagnosis, testing decisions, and prescriptions. Coaches don’t prescribe or change prescribed treatment. You can request an additional telemedicine review with the doctor, and physician fees are confirmed in writing before care is arranged.

Does the program include prescribed treatment?

Not automatically. The doctor decides whether any treatment is appropriate, issues the prescription, and sets the monitoring. Coaching helps with the practical side of following the plan. Autonomic Coaching isn’t a pharmacy, so prescribed items are dispensed by registered pharmacies. Their cost is quoted separately.

Can I join from outside South Africa?

Yes. Sessions run on CAT, and the collaborating doctor is based in South Africa. Clients elsewhere coordinate their care with a locally registered physician, and the team will discuss options with that practitioner on request.

Your Privacy & Personal Information

How your information is handled

How is my information handled?

The team handles personal data under South Africa’s POPIA, for delivering the program and coordinating with authorized care providers. Required sharing is explained before you enroll, and you can withdraw optional sharing. Using your identity or story in marketing requires separate permission. You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, subject to legal and clinical record-keeping duties. Details are in the Privacy Policy, and you can contact justin@autonomiccoaching.com with privacy requests.
